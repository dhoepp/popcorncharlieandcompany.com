Special Orders

Personalized Service:

We work with our customers on all manner of popcorn products specially tailored and customized for events such as: Bridal Showers, Wedding Receptions, Graduation Parties, Birthdays, Baby Showers, Small Corporate Events, etc.


We offer bulk bags of popcorn to save you money for large gatherings or individual snack-packs* for your serving convenience.


*Combo Mixes only.


Our QCA Combo is available in a wide variety of colors, and can be customized in most cases to match an event's color scheme.  For special orders of QCA Combo, the minimum order requires 10 gallons or 60 Snack Packs per Sweet Corn color.


•••


We also take special requests.  If you want a flavor that is out of season or only available upon request, we will make it for you!  The only catch is: you have to meet a minimum purchase amount:

$20 for Savory Flavors

$30 for Candied Flavors

$35 for Candied+ Flavors

$40 for Premium Candied Flavors


If any of this seems confusing, just give us a call during business hours and our staff will be glad to help!

Committed to Quality

Quality should never be compromised. No matter what product you choose, you can feel confident that our products are of the highest caliber.

100% Satisfaction Guaranteed

 Our popcorn is guaranteed fresh for one month after the packaged date. We want you to be completely satisfied, so if you experience any issues with the product, please contact us right away so we can address the problem.
